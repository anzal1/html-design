# Brief: Meniscus design system

Product (fake): Meniscus, a hydration app paired with a smart bottle. Tracks sips, sets a daily goal, nudges with reminders.

Register: expressive (consumer app), but neumorphism is kept honest: every control has an edge line, a text label and a double focus ring, so shadow is never the only signal.

Moods considered: rain-washed ceramic basin; sea glass on a windowsill; pool tile at dusk; fogged shower glass; a lab beaker on a steel bench.
Mood picked: rain-washed ceramic basin. Soft pale blue-grey glaze is the one material every raised and pressed shape is "moulded" from, which is exactly what neumorphism needs, and water sits in it naturally.

Palette (light / dark), each from the basin scene:
- Glaze ground #DDE7EC / #15232C (wet ceramic / basin at night)
- Shadow-dark #A9BAC4 / #0B1217 and shadow-light #F8FCFE / #203542 (the two sides of a moulded edge)
- Ink #0E2430 / #E4F0F5 (dark slate drain cover); muted #3F5A68 / #A9C0CC
- Tidal blue accent #0A5C8C / #6CC1EE (water in deep basin); water fill #1F7FB0 / #3FA4D8
- Edge line #668390 / #5F7A88 (glaze crack line, 3:1 on ground); error #B3261E / #FF9A8F (rust stain)

Type: system rounded stack (ui-rounded, SF Pro Rounded, system-ui) plus ui-monospace for tokens; no network fonts. Scale 13/14/16/20/28/40/display clamp 44-88. Weights 400 and 800, big heavy numbers against small regular labels.

Direction: a calm moulded-ceramic UI where water level is the hero, big numerals on a flat glaze, with contrast, edges and focus solved so it passes WCAG AA.
