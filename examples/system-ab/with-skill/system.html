Design system 1.0

Moulded from one glaze.

Every raised and pressed shape in Meniscus is cut from a single ground colour. Shadows carry depth. Edges, labels and a double focus ring carry meaning, so nothing depends on a soft shadow alone.

Elevation

Three raised levels, two inset levels. Every interactive surface also gets a 1px edge line (3:1 against the ground), because a shadow pair on its own measures about 1.2:1 and fails.

Making neumorphism accessible

The usual failures, and the token that fixes each.

Faint shapes

--color-edge draws a 1px outline on every control. 3:1 or better in both themes.

Invisible focus

--focus-ring is a ground-coloured gap plus a 3px ring in --color-focus. It reads on raised, inset and accent surfaces.

State by shadow only

Selected, on and error each add a second signal: a tick, a text label, an icon or a colour fill.

Disabled looks normal

Disabled controls drop to --opacity-disabled, lose their shadow and get a dashed edge.

Motion

Short, ease-out, no bounce. Hover or focus a block to see its duration. With reduced motion on, durations collapse to zero and the water stops rippling.
