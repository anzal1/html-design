# Brief: Scribbl, a hand-drawn notes app

Register: expressive (consumer app, brief says expressive and hand drawn).

Moods considered: school desk with a pencil case; cafe sketchbook; bedroom wall of sticky notes; laundromat bulletin board; chalkboard menu; tailor's pattern table with tracing paper.
Mood picked: tailor's pattern table (not the first idea, which was sketchbook). Why: tracing paper, fountain-pen ink, tailor's chalk and red basting thread give a hand-marked look with a real palette instead of the usual cream-and-brown notebook.

Palette (light / dark):
- Ground: tracing paper #F4F5F1 / dark: cutting-mat green-black #14171A
- Paper card: pattern paper #FFFFFF / #1E2226
- Ink: fountain-pen indigo #232C73 / chalk-blue #AEB8FF (text and lines)
- Accent: red basting thread #D8402E / #FF7A66
- Tint 1: highlighter yellow #F7E26B / dark #5C5220 (tailor's marker)
- Tint 2: tailor's chalk mint #BFE6D8 / #1F4A3F ; Tint 3: pin-cushion pink #F6C9C4 / #5A2A2A

Type: Caveat (display, wordmark, titles; 700, sizes 28/40/64), Patrick Hand (body and labels 400; 16/18/20). Nothing under 14px.
Direction: a pattern-maker's table where every border wobbles, notes are taped and tilted pieces of paper, and red thread is the one brand line.
