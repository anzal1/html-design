Lisbon in October

trips · edited today, 8:12 · 3 friends can see this

Four days, no rushing. Aim: walk everywhere uphill once, eat custard tarts daily, and be back by the Sunday evening flight.

Each day

  • Thu: tram 28, then Graça viewpoint at sunset
  • Fri: Belém, Pastéis de Belém before 10am
  • Sat: Sintra day trip, Pena Palace on the 9:10 train
  • Sun: Feira da Ladra, one last bifana
Pack the blue raincoat. Locals say October weather "changes its mind" by lunch.

Eat list

Manteigaria for tarts, Taberna da Rua das Flores (arrive at 6:45, no bookings), and a tiny place near Santa Apolónia Rita swears has the best grilled sardines. Budget about €45 a day for food.
