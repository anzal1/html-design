# Brief: Vauren hero (isometric exploded movement)

Brand: Vauren, a fake independent manufacture (Jura, Switzerland, est. 1934). Product: the Calibre 7 skeleton, hand-wound.

**Register:** expressive (brand/marketing). Isometric piece is the centrepiece; type is Swiss-editorial, heavy display vs small light labels.

**Moods considered:** watchmaker's bench under a loupe lamp; glacier hut at dawn; lacquered dial in a vitrine; railway clock-room at night; tidal pool at slack water; limestone cellar vault.
**Mood picked:** limestone cellar vault x ruby bearing (not the bench, which was the first idea): pale cut stone ground, a single ruby jewel as the one accent, brass and steel only inside the illustration.
